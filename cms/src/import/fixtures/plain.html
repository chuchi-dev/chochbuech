Grandma's Zopf

Sunday morning is not complete without a Zopf.

Ingredients

  • 500 g Zopfmehl
  • 1 TL Salz
  • 1½ TL Zucker
  • 20 g Hefe
  • 60 g Butter
  • 3 dl Milch
  • 1 Ei, zum Bestreichen

Method

Mix flour, salt and sugar. Crumble the yeast and add the butter and the lukewarm milk.

Knead for 10 minutes, cover and let it rise for 1 hour.

Braid, brush with egg and bake at 200°C for 35 minutes.
